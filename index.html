Clip Machine

Clip Machine is a personal desktop tool. It cuts the highlights out of gameplay recordings, turns them into short vertical clips, and posts them on a schedule to its owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

It runs on the owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.
